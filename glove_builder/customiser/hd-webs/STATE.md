# HD webatelier — public image studies

24 generated material studies: eight webs, each from the back, palm and thumb.
These images and their interactive draft part maps are published for visual review.
They do not replace the configurable renderer, catalogue thumbnails or order data.

Independent material-study review passed its bounded scope. Exact part masks remain
NEEDS_WORK. Review notes are displayed beside affected views. See EVALUATION-R1.md
and EVALUATION-R2.md for the two completed rounds; publishing the study is not
approval of its construction, recolouring masks or factory accuracy.

Known work: precise palm/web boundaries, separate Back 7 and 8, badge boundaries,
SMLEE motif consistency, the Closed Diamond palm seam and excess lower thumb knots.
Five thumb comparison exports contain only the H-web body; the viewer identifies
those comparisons. Palm and thumb geometry are estimates.

The 24 previews total approximately 1.4 MB. Full originals load individually.
Original image dimensions are shown in the viewer (about 1.57 megapixels, not 8K).
Prompts are in GENERATIONS.json with reference filenames; local computer paths
are omitted. Original photos and user traces are preserved outside this package.
